How it works

Three steps.
One complete summary.

Our AI pipeline retrieves, reads, and summarizes any Swiss Federal Court decision — in your language of choice.

Step 01

Reference Lookup

Enter any BGer or BGE case number. Our system immediately locates the official decision from the Swiss Federal Court repository at bger.ch.

Step 02

Full Text Analysis

The complete decision text is extracted and analyzed by our AI model, identifying the key facts, legal reasoning, and relevant statutory articles.

Step 03

Structured Summary

You receive a clear, structured summary in IT, DE or FR — covering the facts, the court's ruling, and a list of applicable legal articles.

Beta Feature

Legal Summarization is currently under active development. AI-generated summaries may not always capture every nuance of a decision. Always verify critical findings against the original judgment on the official Swiss Federal Court repository.
