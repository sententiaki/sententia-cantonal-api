How it works

Three steps.
One powerful search.

Our AI pipeline translates, searches, and summarizes Swiss legal cases — all in seconds.

Step 01

Multilingual Translation

Your keywords are automatically translated into German and French to ensure the broadest possible coverage across Swiss Federal Court decisions.

Step 02

Targeted Search

Using the Google Custom Search API, we query the official Bger.ch repository and retrieve the five most relevant judgments matching your query.

Step 03

AI Summarization

Each result is processed by AI to generate a concise decision summary highlighting key facts, relevant legal articles, and the court's ruling.

Beta Feature

Smart Search is currently under active development. Results may not always be fully accurate or complete. We recommend verifying important findings directly on the official Swiss Federal Court repository.
